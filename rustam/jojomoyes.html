About me

Hello! Welcome to my website. You probably already know, but I’m Jojo Moyes and I write novels and screenplays. Here you will be able to find out a bit more about me and my books, and any up to date news, such as tour dates.

My brand new novel Someone Else’s Shoes is out now. It’s a story of mix-ups, mess-ups and making the most of second chances. I really hope you enjoy reading it as much as I have loved creating it.
